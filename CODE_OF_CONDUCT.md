# Code of conduct

Be respectful, clear and constructive in issues, pull requests and project spaces.
People with different backgrounds and levels of experience are welcome.

- Critique ideas and code, without personal attacks or harassment.
- Respect privacy. Do not share another person's identifying information, wallet
  links, private messages or security-sensitive material without permission.
- Avoid discriminatory language, threats, sexual harassment and repeated unwanted
  contact.
- Accept feedback and respect moderation decisions.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict
participation to protect contributors. These expectations apply to maintainers too.
Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive content. For a private project-specific
concern, ask a maintainer for a private channel without posting the sensitive details.
